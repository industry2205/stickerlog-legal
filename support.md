# Tapestory — Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email **borabada77@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you. When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version and what you were doing when it happened (Settings › Contact us in the app fills these in for you).

## Frequently asked

**How do I make a page?**  
On the Desk, tap today's card to start a page for that day. Open the sticker drawer to place stickers, papers and tape, then drag, pinch and rotate to arrange them. Your page saves as you go.

**Where do new kits come from?**  
New kits come out over time, and each new featured kit is free for everyone for its first 7 days. Turn on Settings › New kit alerts to hear when one comes out.

**A kit is locked. How do I open it?**  
Join Tapestory Club to open every kit, or watch a short video to open a kit for a few hours (a few passes a day; new passes at midnight). Stickers you placed stay on your page after a pass ends.

**How do I restore my Club purchase?**  
Open Tapestory Club from Settings and tap Restore, using the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

**How do I cancel Tapestory Club?**  
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Tapestory. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

**Can I go back to an earlier version of a page?**  
Yes. Settings › Page history lists your pages; pick one to see its earlier versions and restore the one you want.

**I deleted a page by mistake.**  
Tap Undo right after deleting. Deleted pages and notebooks are kept for 30 days before they are removed for good.

**How do I back up my journal?**  
The line at the top of Settings shows whether iCloud is keeping your journal. To take a copy any time, use Settings › Export all pages: it makes one file with every page and photo that you can save to Files or share.

**Can I keep my journal private?**  
Turn on Settings › App lock. Tapestory then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode whenever you come back to it.

**How do I turn off sounds, vibration or alignment guides?**  
Settings › Alignment guides, Sound effects and Haptics.
