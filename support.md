# Vinette — Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email **borabada77@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you. When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version and what you were doing when it happened (Settings › Help & about › Contact us in the app fills these in for you).

## Frequently asked

**How do I make a page?**  
On the Desk, tap today's card to start a page for that day. Open the sticker drawer to place stickers, papers and tape, then drag, pinch and rotate to arrange them. Your page saves as you go.

**Where do new kits come from?**  
New kits come out over time: they're part of Vinette Club, and you can use one of their stickers for each short video you watch (up to 8 videos a day). Turn on Settings › Notifications › New kit alerts to hear when one comes out.

**A kit is locked. How do I open it?**  
Join Vinette Club to open every kit, or watch a short video to use 1 sticker from any locked kit (up to 8 videos a day; new videos at midnight). Stickers you place stay on your page.

**How do I restore my Club purchase?**  
In Settings, tap Restore purchases, just under Vinette Club. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

**How do I cancel Vinette Club?**  
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Vinette. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

**Can I go back to an earlier version of a page?**  
Yes. Settings › Your pages › Page history lists your pages; pick one to see its earlier versions and restore the one you want.

**I deleted a page by mistake.**  
Tap Undo right after deleting. Deleted pages and diaries are kept for 30 days before they are removed for good.

**How do I back up my diary?**  
Vinette backs up your pages to your own iCloud Drive once a day (iCloud Drive › Vinette in the Files app) — it's on unless you turn it off. In Settings › Your pages › iCloud backup you can turn Back up every day off, tap Back up now, or restore a backup, including one made on another iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. To take a copy any time, use Settings › Your pages › Export all pages: it makes one file with every page and photo that you can save to Files or share.

**Can I keep my diary private?**  
Turn on Settings › Privacy › App lock. Vinette then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode whenever you come back to it.

**How do I turn off sounds, vibration or alignment guides?**  
Settings › Decorating › Alignment guides, Sound effects and Haptics.
